\subsection{Read Outcomes and Disconnection Scenarios}
\label{Section:ValidationPlanUS002}
Related story: \UserStoryRef{US-002}; candidates:
\RequirementRef{REQ-F-004}; \RequirementRef{REQ-F-005}; \RequirementRef{REQ-F-006}; \RequirementRef{REQ-F-007}; \RequirementRef{REQ-F-011}; \RequirementRef{REQ-F-012}; \RequirementRef{REQ-F-013}; \RequirementRef{REQ-Q-002}; \RequirementRef{REQ-D-002}; planned tests:

Scenarios cover positive read requests, completed/partial/empty/failed
reads, timeout continuation, disconnection without crash or host-read-byte
corruption, clear interruption reporting without a completeness claim,
and automatic reconnection/resumption. Device-resident bytes and bytes
generated while disconnected are outside the preservation guarantee.
Reporting mechanism, timeout duration, detailed recovery contracts and
quantitative targets remain TBD (decision co-owners: Joel Murphy and Jason Lefler). Execution and
stakeholder disposition remain pending. See
\hyperref[Section:DraftStoryTraceability]{project traceability}.

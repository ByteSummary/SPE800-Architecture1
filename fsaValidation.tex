whether the outcome meets the user story. Test records support that judgment.
Joel Murphy and Jason Lefler own pending decisions and validation disposition.

details under the \hyperref[Section:ProjectAcquisitionConditions]{agreed
acquisition conditions}. For requirement and test mappings, see the
